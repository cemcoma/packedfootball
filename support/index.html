Common questions

A match looked wrong — a goal that shouldn't have counted, a player stuck, a frozen replay

On the match result screen tap "Something went wrong? Report it", pick what happened and describe it. The report is linked to that exact match, so we can replay it move for move and see what you saw.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password. A reset link goes to your email (check spam). Set the new password on that link's page, then sign in with it.

How do I change my manager name?

Settings → Manager name → Save Name. Names are unique across all players, 3–16 characters, letters, numbers, spaces, underscores, dots and dashes.

I bought Bucks but they didn't arrive

Bucks are added once Apple or Google confirms the payment, which usually takes seconds. If they are still missing after a few minutes, restart the game; if that doesn't help, email us with your manager name and the store receipt (Apple: Settings → your name → Media & Purchases → Purchase History). Refunds themselves are handled by the store: reportaproblem.apple.com for Apple, Google Play refunds for Android.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete Account, then type DELETE to confirm. Your squad, every card, your currencies, your tournament seat and your sign-in are removed for good, and purchases cannot be restored. If you can't get into the game, email us from the address on the account and we'll delete it for you. What is kept and why is in the Privacy Policy.

Can I play on the web?

Yes — the same game runs in the browser with the same account. Real-money purchases are only available in the iOS and Android apps.

Are the ads required?

No. Rewarded videos are optional — watch one to earn a reward, or don't. Nothing in the game is locked behind ads.

Daily tournaments

Each day you play up to ten matches in a group of managers from your league tier. Finish high enough and you're promoted, finish low and you're relegated; every position pays out, and playing all ten matches earns an extra reward you claim from the tournament screen. Standings settle at the day boundary.

Weekly tournaments

Each week you play up to fifty matches in a group of managers from your league tier. Finish high enough and you're promoted, finish low and you're relegated; every position pays out, and playing all fifty matches earns an extra reward you claim from the tournament screen. Standings settle at the week boundary; every Monday at 12.00 pm UTC+3.
